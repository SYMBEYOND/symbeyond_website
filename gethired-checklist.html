Patterns that flag it
✓
Same-length bullets
If all bullets start with a verb and have the same rhythm, vary at least 3 of them.
✓
Zero contractions
Add "I'm" instead of "I am." Add "didn't" instead of "did not." Real people use contractions.
✓
Vague numbers
"Increased sales" becomes "increased sales 18% over 4 months." Add YOUR actual numbers.
✓
No specific names or places
AI avoids specifics. Add the actual company name, city, or tool you used.
✓
Overly formal summary
If your summary sounds like a LinkedIn ad, rewrite it like you're talking to someone at a job fair.
✓
Every sentence is perfect
Real people have a slightly uneven voice. Read it out loud. If it sounds too polished, rough one sentence up a little.
