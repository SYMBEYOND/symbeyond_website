Demo Prompts

Copy-ready prompts for Claude, ChatGPT, Meta AI, or Grok. Tap to copy, paste and go.

Build your resume
Demo 1 — Build a Resume
Use this if you don't have internet access to symbeyond.ai/resume. Replace the brackets with your real info.
I'm applying for a [JOB TITLE] at [TYPE OF COMPANY]. Here is my work history: [PASTE YOUR EXPERIENCE HERE] Here is the job description: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION HERE] Write me a clean, ATS-friendly resume. Simple formatting, no tables or columns.
Tip: Works the same in any AI. Replace the brackets with your real info.
Demo 2 — Humanize the Draft
Run this after building your resume to remove AI-sounding language.
Here is my AI-generated resume draft: [PASTE RESUME HERE] Rewrite it so it sounds like a real person wrote it. Keep all the facts exactly the same. Remove corporate buzzwords. Use plain, direct language. No em dashes.
Tip: Run this every time. It catches patterns that AI detection tools flag.
Cover letters
Demo 3 — Cover Letter
Short, honest, specific. Not a form letter.
Write me a short cover letter for this job: [PASTE JOB POSTING] About me: [2-3 sentences about your experience and why you want this job] Keep it under 200 words. Sound like a real person. No corporate language. No em dashes.
Interview prep
Demo 4 — Practice Interview Questions
Prepare for the questions they will actually ask.
I have an interview for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE]. Give me the 10 most likely interview questions for this role. Then give me a short example answer for each one. Keep the answers honest and direct — not polished corporate speak.
Demo 5 — Explain a Gap
If you have time off your resume, here's how to address it honestly.
Help me answer the question "What were you doing during [TIME PERIOD]?" The truth is: [EXPLAIN WHAT YOU WERE ACTUALLY DOING — family, health, caregiving, moving, anything] Write me an honest, confident answer that doesn't over-explain or apologize. Keep it under 4 sentences.
LinkedIn
Demo 6 — LinkedIn About Section
Most people skip this. It matters.
Write a LinkedIn About section for me. My background: [2-3 sentences about your work history and skills] What I'm looking for: [type of role or industry] One thing I'm proud of: [a specific accomplishment] Keep it under 150 words. First person. Sound like a real person talking, not a resume.
