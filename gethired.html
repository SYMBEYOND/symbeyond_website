Know the signs
Job scam warning

Scammers target people who are actively looking for work. If you see any of these — stop. Do not apply, do not click, do not pay.

They ask you to pay
Real employers never charge to apply, train, or hire.
Hired without an interview
Got the job after only a text? That is not real.
They send you a check
They ask you to send part back. It will bounce.
Too much money offered
Mystery shopper, reshipping — common scam covers.
Pressure to decide now
They need your SSN before you have signed anything.
Strange links or emails
Gmail instead of a company address is a red flag.
