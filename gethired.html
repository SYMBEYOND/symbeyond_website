Phone tip
Your phone can type for you
No keyboard? No problem. Every iPhone and Android has a built-in microphone on the keyboard. Tap it and talk. It types what you say.
1
Tap any text box (like in Resume Builder)
2
Look for the microphone icon on your keyboard
3
Tap it and speak normally. It types as you talk.

Job scam warning signs
Know before you apply
These are not real job offers
Scammers target people who are actively looking for work. If you see any of these, do not apply, do not click, do not pay.
💸
They ask you to pay
Real employers never charge you to apply, for training, for a background check, or for equipment. If they ask for money, it is a scam.
💬
Hired without an interview
You got the job after only a text chat, or with no interview at all. Real employers talk to you first.
🏦
They send you a check to deposit
They mail or text you a check and ask you to send part of it back, or buy gift cards. The check will bounce and you lose the money.
🔗
Strange links or unknown sites
The job posting links to a site you have never heard of, or the email comes from a personal Gmail or Yahoo account instead of a company address.
🤑
Way too much money
The pay sounds too good for the work described. Mystery shopper, package reshipping, and "brand ambassador" roles are common scam covers.
⚡
Pressure to decide right now
They say the offer expires today, or they need your Social Security number and bank account before you have signed anything or met anyone.
If something feels off, it probably is. Search the company name plus the word "scam" before you do anything. Real jobs will still be there after you check.
