The Situation

Oxygen Classic users are at a crossroads

Oxygen 6 just dropped at $399.99. If you are deciding whether to upgrade or move to Elementor, the migration question is real -- and the answer has always been "yes, but it is manual work."

01

The builder lock-in problem

Oxygen stores everything in its own format. Moving to Elementor means starting with a blank screen and rebuilding page by page.

02

Content is the real asset

Your text, images, section structure, and media library have real value. They should not require a full rebuild to carry over.

03

No automated tool exists

There is no "Oxygen to Elementor converter" on the market. Every migration is manual, expensive, and scoped per page.

The Process

Private tooling. Public outcome.

SYMB-OXE is not a SaaS product. It is a private migration workflow built and operated by SYMBEYOND. Your site content is processed locally and never stored on third-party servers.

What this service is

You send a public Oxygen-built WordPress page URL. I run the migration pipeline: content extraction, media download and conversion to WebP, Elementor JSON generation, and media ID remapping for your destination WordPress environment.

You receive an Elementor-importable foundation with your content, images, and section structure carried over. Your designer or developer handles the visual polish -- header, footer, animations, button styling, responsive refinement.

The code stays private. The outcome is yours.
